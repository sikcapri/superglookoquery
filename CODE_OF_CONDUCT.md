# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project touches two things people tend to feel strongly, and vulnerably,
about: personal health data and the devices that manage a chronic condition.
Contributors and users come to it from very different places — some are
here purely as developers, others are managing their own or a family
member's diabetes day to day. Everyone interacting with this project is
expected to help make it a respectful, harassment-free experience for both.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and device/therapy
  choices — there is no one "right" way to manage diabetes, and this project
  is explicitly built to be device-agnostic rather than assume one is best.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and the project's
  users, many of whom depend on their data being handled carefully.
- Showing empathy: a bug report or a confusing setting is often coming from
  someone dealing with a hard day, not just a hypothetical edge case.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in any form.
- Publishing others' private information (including anyone's real health
  data, screenshots containing it, or credentials) without explicit
  permission — this applies doubly here, given what this project handles.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/sikcapri/superglookoquery/issues) or
contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, with the reporter's privacy respected
throughout.
